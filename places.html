Eco Cave Gardens

A cluster of 6 interconnected natural animal-shaped rocky caves (Tiger Cave, Panther Cave, Bat Cave) with hanging gardens and musical fountain. A must for families.

Tiffin Top (Dorothy's Seat)

Located on Ayarpatta hill at 2,292m. Known for 360-degree views of Nainital town and surrounding green Kumaon hills. Popular for hiking and horse riding.

🥾 4 km Forest Trek 🐎 Horse Riding 🌅 Sunset Point

High Altitude Himalayan Zoo

One of only three high altitude zoos in India at 2,100m. Home to endangered Himalayan wildlife including the Royal Bengal Tiger, Snow Leopard, Tibetan Wolf, and Himalayan Black Bear.

🐾 Snow Leopard 🦅 Himalayan Monal 🛺 Shuttle Service
