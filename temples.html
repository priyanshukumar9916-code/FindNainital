51 Shakti Peeth
⏱️ 05:00 AM - 09:00 PM 🙏 Aarti: 7 AM & 7:30 PM

Maa Naina Devi Temple

One of the 51 revered Shakti Peeths of India, located at the northern edge of Naini Lake. Legend has it that the eyes (Nayan) of Goddess Sati fell here, giving the town its name 'Nainital'.

✨ 51 Shakti Peeth 🌊 Naini Lake Shore 🪔 Grand Nanda Devi Fair

World Famous Ashram
⏱️ 06:30 AM - 07:00 PM 📍 17 km on Bhowali Road

Kainchi Dham (Neem Karoli Baba)

The world-famous spiritual sanctuary of saint Neem Karoli Baba (Maharaj-ji). Renowned for inner peace and miracle blessings, attracting devotees worldwide including Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg, and Virat Kohli.

🕊️ Divine Meditation 🍲 Free Prasad (Kichadi) 🎉 15th June Foundation Day
🚗 Round Trip Cab (₹1,400)
Sunset & Darshan
⏱️ 05:00 AM - 08:30 PM 🏔️ Altitude: 1,951 m

Hanuman Garhi Temple

Built by Neem Karoli Baba in 1950, housing a magnificent 70-foot standing statue of Lord Hanuman displaying Lord Rama in his chest. Renowned as the most peaceful sunset viewpoint in the valley.

🌅 Glorious Sunset View 🔭 ARIES Observatory Nearby 🐒 Peaceful Grounds
🚗 Book Cab (₹600)
350-Yr Ancient Shrine
⏱️ 06:00 AM - 07:30 PM 📍 50 km from Nainital

Mukteshwar Dham (Lord Shiva)

An ancient 350-year-old Lord Shiva temple atop a 2,312m high peak. Adjacent to Chauli Ki Jali, a natural cliff with rock lattice offering sweeping 180-degree Himalayan views from Nanda Devi to Trishul.

🔱 350-Year Shivling 🧗 Chauli Ki Jali Cliff 🍎 Apple Orchards Route
🚗 Day Tour Cab (₹2,800)
Natural Stone Icon
⏱️ 06:00 AM - 08:00 PM 📍 Thandi Sadak, Naini Lake

Pashan Devi Temple

A natural rock formation temple representing the nine forms of Goddess Durga (Navdurga) bathed by the waters of Naini Lake along the peaceful pedestrian promenade of Thandi Sadak.

🌿 Navdurga Stone Relief 🚶 Walking Promenade 💧 Lake Water Touch
🚗 Book Cab (₹500)
God of Justice
⏱️ 06:00 AM - 07:00 PM 📍 16 km near Bhowali

Golu Devta Temple (Ghorakhal)

Dedicated to Lord Golu, the supreme presiding deity of Justice in Kumaon. Covered with thousands of brass bells and written stamp-paper petitions offered by devotees seeking divine justice and wish fulfillment.
