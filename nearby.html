Largest Lake in Kumaon

Bhimtal Lake & Island

Named after the Mahabharata legend Bhima. Boasts a charming island in the center with an aquarium cafe reachable by boat. Less crowded and larger than Naini Lake.

🏝️ Island Aquarium Cafe 🚣 Boating & Zorbing 🧱 1883 Victorian Masonry Dam
🚗 Book Cab (₹1,400)
Pristine Nature
📍 24 km (50 Mins Drive) 🏔️ Altitude: 1,370 m

Sattal (Cluster of 7 Lakes)

An idyllic interconnected cluster of 7 natural freshwater lakes (Ram, Sita, Laxman, Panna, Nal-Damyanti, Sukha, Garud Tal) enveloped by dense oak and pine woods.

🚣 Kayaking & Rafting ⛺ Forest Camping 🦋 Butterfly Museum
🚗 Book Cab (₹1,600)
Nine-Cornered Lake
📍 26 km (55 Mins Drive) 🏔️ Altitude: 1,220 m

Naukuchiatal (Lake of 9 Corners)

The deepest lake in the region with 9 distinct corners. Legend says anyone who sees all 9 corners from one spot attains enlightenment. Famous for tandem paragliding.

🪂 Paragliding Flights 🎣 Angling & Yachting 🌿 Peaceful Valley Slopes
🚗 Book Cab (₹1,600)
Apple Orchards & Peaks
📍 50 km (1.5 Hrs Drive) 🏔️ Altitude: 2,312 m

Mukteshwar & Chauli Ki Jali

A hilltop paradise offering unhindered 180-degree Himalayan views from Nanda Devi to Trishul. Famous for apple and peach orchards, 350-yr Shiva Temple, and Chauli Ki Jali cliff.

🧗 Rock Rappelling 🍎 Apple & Plum Orchards 🏔️ Majestic Snow Peaks
🚗 Book Day Cab (₹2,800)
Cultural Capital
📍 66 km (2.5 Hrs Drive) 🏔️ Altitude: 1,638 m

Almora & Kasar Devi Temple

The cultural heart of Kumaon. Kasar Devi on Crank's Ridge is located within a geomagnetic cosmic radiation field (like Machu Picchu and Stonehenge), celebrated for deep meditation.

🧘 Cosmic Energy Field 🍬 Authentic Bal Mithai 🏛️ Heritage Cobblestone Streets
🚗 Book Outstation Cab (₹3,500)
Cantonment Charm
📍 60 km (2.2 Hrs Drive) 🏔️ Altitude: 1,869 m

Ranikhet (Queen's Meadow)

Serene cantonment hill station founded by the British. Features the 9-hole Asia's highest natural Golf Course, Chaubatia fruit gardens, and Kumaon Regimental Centre Museum.

⛳ 9-Hole Golf Grounds 🌲 Chaubatia Fruit Gardens 🎖️ KRC Army Heritage
🚗 Book Outstation Cab (₹3,200)
