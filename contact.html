FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Nainital Travel Guidelines & FAQs

🚗 Are commercial vehicles allowed on Mall Road?

Mall Road is closed for heavy vehicles from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM during peak tourist season to keep the lakeside promenade pedestrian-only.

❄️ When can I see snowfall in Nainital?

Snowfall in Nainital, Kilbury, and Snow View Point generally occurs between late December and early February.

🙏 What is the best time to visit Kainchi Dham?

Kainchi Dham is open year-round from 6:30 AM to 7:00 PM. Morning darshan between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM is the most peaceful before highway traffic builds up.
